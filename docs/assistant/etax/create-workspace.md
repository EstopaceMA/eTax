# Create an eTax workspace

Create a workspace when you are new to eTax or when you need a fresh filing preparation account.

## Steps

1. Go to the eTax sign-up page.
2. Choose the taxpayer category that best describes you.
3. Enter your full name.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Create a password.
6. Submit the form to create your workspace.

## Taxpayer categories

eTax asks for a taxpayer category so it can set a sensible starting profile.

- Employee / compensation earner: salary or wages from an employer.
- Self-employed professional / freelancer: direct income from clients, platforms, commissions, professional practice, or freelance work.
- Single proprietor: registered individual business owner.
- Mixed-income earner: employment income plus business, freelance, or professional income.
- E.O. 98 / one-time transaction: TIN or tax record for a government, bank, estate, donor, or one-time transaction.
- Non-individual taxpayer: corporation, partnership, cooperative, association, or similar organization.

## After sign-up

After your workspace is created, eTax saves your starting profile and opens the protected app area. Review your Tax profile page before relying on generated filing documents.
