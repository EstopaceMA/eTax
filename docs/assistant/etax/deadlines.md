# Check filing deadlines

The Deadlines page shows upcoming compliance dates saved in eTax.

## What you can see

- Deadline title.
- Due date.
- Deadline status.
- Days remaining or days overdue.
- Filing description.
- Filing channel.

## How to use it

1. Open **Deadlines** from the app navigation.
2. Review the nearest due dates first.
3. Use the status and days-away indicator to prioritize what needs attention.
4. Open **Filing tracker** when you are ready to work on a filing period.
5. Open **Documents** if required preparation items are still missing.

## Reminder

Deadlines help you prepare early. Always verify official filing dates and requirements when submitting to a government channel.
