# Preview and download a BIR form

Use the BIR Form tab inside Filing tracker to review the generated PDF for the selected filing period.

## Steps

1. Open **Filing tracker**.
2. Select the filing period.
3. Open the **BIR Form** tab.
4. Review the PDF preview.
5. Use the download button if you need a local copy.

## What eTax fills

eTax draws known profile details into the PDF when those details are available in your workspace.

## What may stay blank

Financial fields can remain blank for manual review when eTax does not have enough saved information to fill them confidently.

## Before filing

Always review the PDF before submitting it through the official filing channel. Make sure profile details, income totals, and any manually completed fields are correct.
