# Track filing documents

The Documents page is a preparation checklist for filing requirements.

## Steps

1. Open **Documents** from the app navigation.
2. Review each checklist item.
3. Mark an item complete when you already have it.
4. Leave or mark an item missing when you still need to prepare it.
5. Return to the dashboard to see how document readiness affects your overall preparation status.

## Typical checklist items

The checklist can include taxpayer identification records, income summaries, receipts, invoices, certificates, and prior filing or payment references.

## Difference from invoice uploads

The Documents page tracks whether required documents are ready. To upload invoices or income records for extraction, use **Filing tracker** instead.
